# Simulation Reports

[`sim-baseline.md`](sim-baseline.md) is generated by the `sim` module. Don't edit it by hand. Regenerate it with:

```bash
./gradlew :sim:run --args="--seeds 200 --out reports/sim-baseline.md"
```

The simulator is deterministic: the same code and the same arguments give the same file. A diff of the report therefore shows exactly what a rules or content change did. Commit the regenerated report together with the change that caused it.

To read one fight blow by blow, trace it by operative, deck, encounter and fight number:

```bash
./gradlew :sim:run -q --args="--trace oracle/starter/the_rent/3"
```

## What the simulator does

- **Matrix:** every operative (Vowknight, Oracle, Splinter) with two decks fights every encounter.
  - The *starter* deck is the 10 cards from [docs/03](../docs/03-factions-and-operatives.md).
  - The *mid* deck adds 8 of that faction's sample cards.
- **Fights:** single combats at full HP and Paradox 0, with no relics, Imprints or rewards.
- **Bots:**
  - `greedy` tries every candidate action on a copy of the state, with the unseen part of the Future reshuffled and fresh dice, so it can't peek. It plays the best one by a hand-tuned evaluation, or ends the turn when nothing beats ending it.
  - `random` picks uniformly among legal actions. It is the floor.

## Baseline findings

These come from the first baseline: October 2026, the prototype numbers straight from docs 03 and 04, 200 fights per cell, 33,600 fights in all. They are proposals. Nothing below has been changed yet.

### 1. Blank can soft-lock a fight (design bug)

**What the sims show.** The Oracle's starter deck stalls at the 60-round cap:

- against **The Rent** in 52 of 200 fights
- against **Loose Threads** in 16 of 200

The Splinter's starter deck stalls against The Rent in 7 of 200.

**Why.** Blank lasts the whole combat, and Unpick-only Tear enemies (the Loose End) deal no damage. Once every damage card is Blank, neither side can win. The Vowknight never stalls because its Strike always deals damage.

**Proposal.** A Blank card is restored when your Past is reshuffled into your Future: the thread re-knits. The Tear still costs you draws, but a fight always moves.

### 2. The Vowknight is about twice as efficient as the others

**Greedy bot, starter decks:**

| Operative | Wins | Average rounds | Max HP lost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vowknight | 100% | 2.7 | 5% |
| Splinter | 100% | 3.7 | 10% |
| Oracle | 96% | 4.1 | 11% |

Even the random bot wins 99% of its fights with the Vowknight.

**Why.** Strike is 4 free damage every turn, and 8 with *Vow of the Sword*. That is worth more than a card per turn. Forecast and Shift are worth far less.

**Proposals.** The docs call the Vowknight the most forgiving operative, so some lead is intended. To narrow the gap, try either:

- *Vow of the Sword* at +2 instead of +4
- a stronger Forecast, for example Foresee 2 that also draws 1 when you send a card to the bottom

Then re-run and compare.

### 3. Most fights are too soft

**Normal fights.**

- Fog on Fleet Street, Glass Desert and Drone Swarm are trivial for every operative and deck: under 10% of max HP lost. Loose Threads is trivial for five of the six.
- The rest cost 5–27%.

**Elites.**

| Elite | Max HP lost |
|---|---|
| The Inquisitor | 7–46% |
| The Rent | 4–14% |
| The Pruner | 2–12% |

**Targets.** HP carries across a run, so a reasonable aim is about 15–25% for a normal fight and 35–50% for an elite.

**First pass to try:**

- **Sandglass Golem:** Charge 10 → 16.
- **Pruner:** a second slot with *Attack 6 ①* every round, next to its charge.
- **The Rent:** 60 → 80 HP, and an *Attack 8 ①* in its loop.
- **Damage-only normal enemies:** about +30% damage.

### 4. Borrow is free on the last turn

**What the sims show.**

- The greedy bot borrows 2.4–3.9 energy per fight.
- About 0.4–0.7 energy per won fight is never repaid, because Debt ends with the combat.

So borrowing on the final turn is always right, and rolling Debt forward costs nothing in the end.

**Proposal.** Debt left at the end of a combat is repaid at the first Dawn of the next combat.

### 5. Delay, draw and setup cards look weak, partly because of the bot

**Delay.** The greedy bot Delays an intent 0–0.27 times per fight.

**Plays per copy per fight:**

| Card | Plays |
|---|---|
| Deterministic Model | 0.00 |
| Hold the Hour | 0.07–0.24 |
| Litany of Steel | 0.08 |
| Elsewhen Guard | 0.10 |
| Shield of the Line | 0.13 |

**Why.** Part of it is the bot: one action of lookahead can't see a two-card combo, or the value of buying a turn to land a kill. Part of it is the content: when enemies hit softly, racing beats defending.

**Proposal.** Re-check after the enemy pass in (3). Before judging these cards, add a stronger bot: two-ply search, or the MCTS bot planned in [docs/09](../docs/09-tech-architecture.md#testing-and-qa).

### 6. Paradox and Unravel aren't exercised yet

**What the sims show.** No fight reached Paradox 10.

**Why.** Single fights start at Paradox 0, and Errata cards add 1 at a time. Paradox is a run-wide meter ([docs/05](../docs/05-run-structure.md#paradox)).

**Proposal.** Add fights seeded at Paradox 6–9, and later, run-level simulations.
